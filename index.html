How it all started

1

A kid with a gamepad

I grew up on platformers. Jumping over pits, collecting coins, hunting for secret rooms and beating “just one more level” long past bedtime.

2

The love never left

Years later I still had that itch. So instead of waiting for the platformer I wanted to play, I started building it myself – evening after evening, level after level.

3

Welcome to Mazan Age

A prehistoric valley of waterfalls, rope bridges and hidden caves – full of grumpy dinosaurs, angry boars and one very stubborn stone golem.

4

And now I need you

The game is coming by the end of 2026. Leave your email to play it first – and if you like what you see, a small one-time tip helps me finish it.
