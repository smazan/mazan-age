How it all started

A kid with a gamepad

I grew up on platformers. Jumping over pits, collecting coins, hunting for secret rooms and playing “just one more level” long past bedtime.

The love never left

Years later I still had that itch. So instead of waiting for the platformer I wanted to play, I started building it myself – evening after evening, level after level.

Welcome to Mazan Age

A prehistoric valley of waterfalls, rope bridges and hidden caves – full of grumpy dinosaurs, angry boars and one very stubborn stone golem.

Questions & answers

Mazan Age in short: a 2D single-player platformer set in the Stone Age, made by one developer and planned for release by the end of 2026.

What is Mazan Age?

Mazan Age is a 2D prehistoric platformer made by one person, a lifelong fan of platform games. You play as a cavekid boy or girl and travel through a jungle valley full of dinosaurs, monsters and secrets.

When does Mazan Age come out?

The game is planned for release by the end of 2026. Leave your email on this website and you will get a message on release day.

What can you do in the game?

Jump over pits and rope bridges, climb to higher floors, smash secret rocks, explore ten hidden caves, collect coins, diamonds and food, and fight with 12 stone-age weapons such as clubs, axes, a spear, a boomerang, a slingshot and a fire torch.

Which enemies are in Mazan Age?

Wild boars in groups of three, snakes, mammoths, spiky turtles, spiders, pterodactyls that drop rocks, raptors and a rival caveman. More are coming, for example a stone golem, a saber-toothed tiger and a prehistoric crocodile.

Is Mazan Age a single-player game?

Yes. Mazan Age is a single-player game: you pick one hero, the cavekid boy or the cavekid girl.

How can I support the development?

With a one-time tip of 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 euros on this website, and by subscribing to the YouTube channel @mazanage.
